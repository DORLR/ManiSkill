# 2026-11-08: latent-free baseline (v1) vs latent FM (v1)

## What was compared

Both policies predict the same thing (the next 16 joint targets, execute 8, replan every 0.4 s) from
the same demonstrations (successful expert trajectories of the train split) with the same
flow-matching head and the same 4-frame context. They differ only in how the images are turned into
the policy's input:

* **latent FM (v1)**: images -> frozen v1 VAE -> latent (64 x 8). The VAE was trained first with its
  own losses (point reconstruction, plug-point loss: what the plug still has to move to be inserted).
  The policy only sees the latent.
* **baseline**: images -> the same encoder architecture as the v1 VAE, trained from scratch by the
  policy's behaviour-cloning loss alone. No latent bottleneck, no plug-point / reconstruction loss.

Question answered: does the VAE stage (the shared latent with its pose supervision) help the policy,
or would training the same network end to end do as well?

## Results (ood_val: 13 held-out shapes x 6 grasp conditions x 2 grasps x 2 starts; 12 grasps skipped
## because the expert itself cannot insert them; same 288 episodes for both; 60 s per episode;
## success = plug within 1 mm of full depth)

| condition | baseline | latent FM | episodes |
|---|---|---|---|
| grasp inside the training ranges | 2 | 20 | 50 |
| grasp pitch interpolation | 5 | 15 | 40 |
| grasp pitch extrapolation | 2 | 16 | 52 |
| grasp position interpolation | 3 | 18 | 50 |
| grasp position extrapolation | 2 | 18 | 50 |
| both extrapolated | 2 | 11 | 46 |
| **all** | **16 = 6%** | **98 = 34%** | 288 |

Median time to insert: baseline 30.6 s, latent FM 27.4 s. Validation action error (open loop, ood_val):
baseline best 0.88 mrad (step 45k of 60k), latent FM 0.69 mrad.

## How to read it

* The latent FM inserts about 6x more often. The difference is far beyond chance (each condition has
  40-52 episodes; overall 16 vs 98 of 288).
* The open-loop action error differs much less (0.88 vs 0.69 mrad) than closed-loop success: small
  per-step errors of the baseline accumulate when it acts on its own observations.
* Caveat: the budgets were not equal -- baseline batch 32 x 60k steps, latent FM batch 256 x 100k steps,
  learning rate 1e-4 vs 3e-4. The baseline saw ~13x fewer samples. This alone could explain part of
  the gap; the v3 baseline is therefore trained with exactly the latent policy's budget (effective batch
  256 via 8 accumulation steps, lr 3e-4, 100k steps), which takes ~5 days because every step runs the
  full encoder on 1024 images.
