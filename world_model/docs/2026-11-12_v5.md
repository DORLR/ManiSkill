# v5 (2026-11-12): region tokens in the latent, and the contact set

Decided with the user on 2026-11-12 after the findings of 2026-11-11 (docs/2026-11-10_v4_runs.md):
the latent of crop_ae_v4e is 64 near copies of one vector, and the data contain no long pressed states.
Two independent causes, two changes. Nothing downstream is fine-tuned: every model after the autoencoder
is retrained from scratch once the gates below pass.

## 1. Crop autoencoder v5

What was wrong (read in the code and the training log of crop_ae_v4e, measured on its latents):

* Read-out: 64 learned queries read the 512 crop tokens, and the decoder reads all 64 tokens globally.
  Nothing makes the queries read different places. Measured: token-specific part of the variance 0.4-0.5 %,
  99 % of the frame-to-frame variation in 15 components.
* Loss: the RGB term was a plain mean over pixels (the openings are a few dozen of 16 384) and about 4 % of
  the total (depth about half). The gradient norm was about 100 against a clip of 1.0 for the whole run, so
  the update direction was that of the largest terms.
* Selection: the checkpoint was chosen on the pose score alone, one global quantity.

What changed (`configs/v5/crop_ae.json`, `models/crop_encoder.py`, `models/crop_ae.py`, `training/crop_ae.py`):

| part | v4e | v5 |
|---|---|---|
| read-out | 64 learned queries | none: 2 x 2 neighbouring crop tokens merged; 8 x 8 tokens per crop, 128 in all, each one 16 x 16 px region |
| decoder | 512 patch queries cross-attend to all tokens | each token is split back into its own 2 x 2 crop tokens, then self-attention over the 512 |
| RGB loss | pixel mean, weight 10 | pixels on the UNet's predicted pins / openings (dilated 5 px) count 11 times; weight 40 |
| edge loss | none | L1 between pixel differences of reconstruction and image, same weights; weight 40 |
| perceptual loss | none | LPIPS on AlexNet features, weight 2 (VGG measured at 0.19 s per step on the shared GPU, AlexNet 0.010 s) |
| selection | pose score | pose score + 2 x error across the axis within 5 mm + 10 x RGB error on the key pixels |

Unchanged: the inputs of every crop token, the three attention stages, the crop size and placement, 16 numbers
per token, the pose read-out (per-pixel motion, rigid fit). Not added: masked training (it does not address a
measured fault; one cause at a time).

The UNet's masks are loss weights only (a real-robot quantity, the same prediction that places the crops).

Downstream: `HistoryCDiT(n_tokens=128)` lays the tokens out as a (16, 8) grid, the plug crop's 8 x 8 above the
opening crop's; NWM's CDiT is used as it is (patch size 1, learned position embedding per token). Before v5 the
8 x 8 "image" given to CDiT was an arbitrary order of the 64 queries; now a grid position is a place in a crop.
`SuccessHead`, `VisionJointAdapter`, `LatentFMPolicy` take `n_tokens`.

## 2. The contact set (`collect/explore.py --contact`, data `wm_v1c`)

The first exploration set (wm_v1x, 5575 trajectories, 4.5 s on average) holds the command for 0.4-1.6 s after
the plug is blocked and ends. Closed-loop policies stay pressed on the board for many seconds and re-plan from
where the hand is. The contact set is the same policy-independent exploration (starts nearer the hole: 5 %
aligned, 50 % within 1 mm, 30 % 1-3 mm, 15 % 3-10 mm; fewer maneuvers) followed by 4-16 s at the board:
holds, and "decisions" of 4 / 8 / 16 commands that ramp (or step) from the measured hand position to that
position plus a lead of up to 1.5 mm -- along the axis (push), across it (slide / wiggle / circle / random
search), or backwards (let go, side step). Everything is drawn before the trajectory starts; only FK feedback
is used; decisions start on multiples of 8 control steps.

Two things found on the smoke runs and built in:

* A controller that re-plans every decision from the measured hand position and keeps pushing slides away
  across the board steadily (up to 26 mm in 15 s): the arm's stiffness is not the same in all directions, so
  a push along the axis has a component across it, and each new decision accepts the slip. So 70 % of the
  primitives keep their place across the axis (`anchored`: the leads move a remembered position), 30 % do not
  (that sliding is real and stays in the data).
* The phase stays within about 3 mm (across the axis) of where it began: a lead pointing further out is
  mirrored, from outside a decision only returns. This uses the hand's own positions, not the hole's, so the
  commands still carry no information about where the hole is (checked: `check_contact_set.py`, part D).
